Delete your Healthify data

Last updated: 2026-05-18  ·  App: Healthify (com.DeltaPKR.Healthify)

Healthify is designed so most of your data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app already removes the local database, your reminders, and your check-in history from the phone.

A small amount of data is mirrored to Firebase (cloud) so the same anonymous account can be used across devices. The instructions below cover both the on-device and the cloud copies.

1. Delete the on-device data

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Healthify.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear storage. This wipes the local database (healthify.db), reminders, and any cached Firebase tokens.
  3. (Optional) Tap Uninstall to remove the app itself.

2. Delete the cloud copy

The cloud copy is stored under an anonymous identifier (Firebase UID) — we never associate it with your name, email, or phone number. To have it erased, send an email request:

To: deltapkr.developer@gmail.com

Subject: Healthify data deletion request

Body should include:

  • Your anonymous Firebase UID (find it in the app: open Profile tab — the UID is shown at the bottom of the profile card).
  • Optional: the approximate date you first installed the app, so we can cross-reference if the UID has rotated.

We acknowledge each request within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

3. What gets deleted

Data typeLocationRemoved by
Profile, check-ins, reminders On-device SQLite (healthify.db) Step 1 (Clear storage or Uninstall)
Profile mirror + check-in summaries Firebase Firestore Step 2 (email request)
Anonymous Firebase UID + auth token Firebase Authentication Step 2 (email request)
Crash reports + diagnostics Firebase Crashlytics Auto-purged after 90 days per Firebase policy

4. Data we never collect

For full clarity, the items below were never sent off your device, so they do not need separate deletion:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any government identifiers.
  • Location (precise or approximate).
  • Health Connect raw records — only daily summaries leave the device.
